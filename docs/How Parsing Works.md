# How Surasura Reads Your Content

- What counts as one word — so your list and your cards make sense
- **Right** = hand-checked on a 2,000-file library
- ⚖️ = left as it is, on purpose
- Sentences with a serious mistake: **1 in 9 → 1 in 27**

---

## Japanese

### Compound words are one word

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| 上層 + 部 | 上層部 |
| 走る + 出す | 走り出す |
| 出来 + 損ない | 出来損ない |

- ⚖️ 食べ始める stays two words
- ⚖️ 三年 stays a count
- **Right:** 39 of 40
- **Setting:** Phrases and titles as one word — on

### Words cut at their ending are whole

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| 下る + ない | くだらない 'trivial' |
| 思う + ず | 思わず |
| いつ + も | いつも |
| 何 + か | 何か |

- Knowing いつ and も doesn't make いつも known
- ⚖️ 知らない stays 知る + ない
- ⚖️ 本当に stays 本当 + に
- **Right:** 63 of 64 (くだらない) · about 9 in 10 (いつも)

### Pronouns with a suffix are one word

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| 何 + 様 | 何様 |
| お前 + さん | お前さん |

- ⚖️ あなた方 stays a plural
- **Right:** 98 of 100
- **Setting:** Pronouns with a suffix as one word — on

### Names are one word

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| グリム + ジョー | グリムジョー |
| ミロ + ナイ | ミロナイ |

- ⚖️ Laughs and stutters stay apart: アッハハ, バッバカ
- ⚖️ A surname that is also an everyday word stays that word
- **Right:** 35 of 47
- **Setting:** Katakana names as one word — on
- **Setting:** Ignore names — off

### One-kanji words are on your list

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| 手, 顔, 声 — never listed | listed |
| 年 in 三年 — a use of 年 | a count |
| 目 in ２時間目 — 'eye' | a count |

- ⚖️ A misread kanji can become a row — Ignore it
- ⚖️ 本 'book' stays off (more often a counter or suffix)
- **Right:** 92 of 124 new rows
- **Setting:** List one-kanji words only when they're dictionary words — on

### Idioms get a row of their own

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| 気 + が + する | 気がする |
| 腑 + に + 落ちる | 腑に落ちる |
| 彼のほう 'his side' — counted as 彼の方 'that person' | not an idiom |

- Waits until you know its words: 本題に入る waits for 本題
- ⚖️ The words inside keep their own rows
- ⚖️ A rare idiom Surasura reads as other words is left out: 同病相憐れむ
- **Right:** top 100 rows checked — all 100 kept
- **Setting:** Idioms and set phrases on your list — on

### Numbers and symbols are not words

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| 二十, 百 | not words |
| 祈り－心 — － read as から | a dash |

- ⚖️ ％, ○, × still count: パーセント, 丸, バツ
- **Right:** 18 of 18 dashes

### Sounds and fillers are their word

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| ま + あ | まあ |
| ハァハァハァ | はあはあ |
| ドキッと — a word apart from ドキッ | one word, shown as ドキッと |

- ⚖️ Kansai ええ 'good' counts as ええ 'yes'
- **Right:** 83 of 88 (ええ)

### Every spelling is the same word

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| ﾅｲﾌ | ナイフ |
| すご～い | すごい |
| ジャ～ック | ジャック |

- ⚖️ １人 (wide １) counts as 1 + 人
- **Right:** 46 of 57 stretched words

### Only the story counts

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| [音楽] | not counted |
| アサ： — read as 麻 'hemp' | not counted |
| 漢字《かんじ》 — read as 感じ | 漢字 |
| 「階で待つ」 | 「30階で待つ」 |
| an old Shift_JIS file — 0 words | every line |

- ⚖️ A thought in （ ） in a subtitle never counts
- **Right:** 15 of 16 readings in ( )
- **Setting:** Readings in ( ) in books — Hiragana only

### A quote is one sentence

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| 「今日は晴れ。 / 明日は雨」と言った。 | one sentence |
| a caption split over two lines | one sentence |

- ⚖️ A line break still ends a sentence

### Your Anki card is its word

| Card | Read as |
| :-- | :-- |
| 撒く, with まく on line 2 | 撒く |
| 勉強した | 勉強 |
| お部屋 | 部屋 |
| 心する 'take heed' | 心する, not 心 |
| ひかり 'light' | 光, not the name ヒカリ |

- ⚖️ A kana-only card asks you: まく — 撒く or 膜?

### Words you can read sit lower

- 上層部 when you know 上層 and 部 → half score, never new in a sentence
- The same for an idiom whose words you know
- ⚖️ It keeps its row and its card

## What a big library makes possible

- Your library decides what to learn next — never what a word is
- Names and a story's own words are the exception, each behind its own setting

### New words, found in your sentences

| Sentence | New to you | Becomes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 猫が飛び出した | 飛び出す | an example for 飛び出す |
| 猫が窓から飛び出した | 窓, 飛び出す | not yet — two new words |

- Every sentence is checked against your known words
- Exactly one new word → that word's example (i+1)

### Names your library repeats

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| シャドウ + ガーデン | シャドウガーデン |

- Used 3+ times, and together at least 7 times in 10
- **Right:** 43 of 57
- **Setting:** Names your library repeats as one word — on

### Kanji names

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| 一 + 護 | 一護 |
| 奏 + 汰 | 奏汰 |

- A name in the names dictionary, used 3+ times
- **Right:** 45 of 50
- **Setting:** Kanji names as one word — on

### A story's own words

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| 斬 + 魄 + 刀 | 斬魄刀 |
| 卍 + 解 | 卍解 |

- Used 3+ times, and together at least 7 times in 10
- ⚖️ Auto-made captions don't count
- **Right:** 65 of 78
- **Setting:** Kanji terms your library repeats as one word — on

### Idioms you actually meet

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| 腑に落ちる, met once | no row |
| 腑に落ちる, met often | a row |

- An idiom gets a row once you meet it as often as a word on your list

### A rare compound feeds its words

| Before | Now |
| :-- | :-- |
| 前言撤回 ×2 + 撤回 ×1 → 撤回: 1 use | 撤回: 3 uses |

- ⚖️ A part never used alone gains nothing: 前言
