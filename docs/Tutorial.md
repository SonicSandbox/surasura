# Surasura 3.0: a quick guide

> Surasura shows you the exact words worth your time, in the order you'll meet them.

## 1. Setup (once)

| Page | What you do |
| :-- | :-- |
| **What are you learning?** | Japanese or Chinese |
| **Skip the words you already know** | **From Anki** (it stays in sync), **Import a list** (Migaku, Jiten, a file) or **Start fresh** |
| **Where are your shows and books?** | *Choose a folder…* · *Choose a file…* · *YouTube…*. Nothing is moved: Surasura reads them where they are |
| **Where does each one go?** | Every title, one key each: **Soon (S) · Current (→) · Goal (↓) · Finished (↑) · Ignore (←)**. *Put the rest in…* sorts what's left at once, and *Start sorting over* begins again |
| **Your cards, in the order you'll meet the words** | Let Surasura keep Anki's new cards in your order (順) |
| **Cards that make themselves** | Anki Miner makes a card from the episode you're watching: the line, its audio and a picture |
| **Your list** | Your **Level**: fewer, common words, or more, rarer ones. *Automatic* picks it for you |

- Anki needs the free **AnkiConnect** add-on: **Copy code**, then in Anki *Tools › Add-ons › Get Add-ons…*, paste,
  restart Anki.
- Nothing of your own yet? **Try it with samples first**.

## 2. The window

![Current: Up Next with its cover, % known and new words](assets/images/readme/current.png)

| Tab | What it holds |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Current** | Your order: **Up Next** on top, then the Soon line, and **Goal** (someday, in no order) at its foot |
| **Finished** | What you've finished: a record, and still a source of sentences. **Put back** returns it |
| **Needs you** | One card for each thing that waits on your decision, each with *Not now* · *Stop asking* |
| **Settings** | Every setting on one page; each row says what it does |

- **Move a show:** drag it, or use ↑ ↓. Drop it on **Soon**, **Goal** or **Finished**. One Undo.
- **Pick several:** Ctrl / Shift+click, then drag or ⋯. One Undo moves them all back.
- **Watched an episode?** Check its stone (or Up Next's **✓ Done**): its words leave your list.
- **Search:** `/` or Ctrl+K, by kana, kanji or romaji.
- **+ Add:** files, a folder, an EPUB or Anki deck, a YouTube or bilibili link, or pasted text.
- **The circles in the bottom bar** show what's running. Yellow always means a decision: click it.

## 3. New arrivals

![New arrivals: new shows and episodes, with their covers, waiting to be placed](assets/images/readme/arrivals.png)

- A new episode, book or video in your folders arrives by itself.
- Drag it into your order, or press ↑. *Sort them all…* opens the one-key sort again.

## 4. Your list and your journey

![The journey: each episode's new words with a sentence from it](assets/images/readme/journey.png)

- **Generate** (top) builds your list from your library and the words you know. It shows ✓ when it's up to date.
- **Journey ↗** opens it: each episode's new words, in the order you'll meet them, each with a sentence from your
  library.
- **Level ▾** changes how rare a word can be and still make your list.

## 5. Cards

![Mine: the episode's cards come next](assets/images/readme/mine.png)

- **Mine** makes an episode's cards with Anki Miner. Surasura installs Anki Miner and finishes its setup itself.
- **順 Junban** keeps Anki's new cards in your order: move a show, and Anki follows.
- Desktop Anki must be open. Set your deck's *New card sort order* to **Order gathered**.

## 6. Books and audiobooks

![A book opened by chapter: each chapter's % known and new words; 🎧 its audiobook joined to it](assets/images/readme/book-chapters.png)

- An EPUB opens by chapter. 🎧 An audiobook joins its book's title; its transcript is its text.
