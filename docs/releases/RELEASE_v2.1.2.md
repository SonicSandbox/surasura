# Surasura v2.1.2 - Release Notes

## Summary
- Look a word up online (nadeshiko), the `\` hotkey.

- **A ⌕ Button on Every Card**: A small search icon sits just left of **Ignore** in the card footer.
  Clicking it opens that word on **Nadeshiko** in a new tab, so your place in the report is never
  lost.
- **`\` Does It Without Reaching for the Mouse**: Press `\` and the word you're currently on opens.
  It's in the Hotkeys Reference (the ⓘ in the corner) alongside `v`, `z`, `w` and `s`.


### Two Settings, under **Advanced Settings → 📊 Experience & UI**

- **Turn the Button Off Entirely**: **Show word lookup button (⌕)** — on by default. Switched off,
  the icon isn't drawn at all, and `\` stops appearing in the Hotkeys Reference rather than being
  listed as a key that does nothing.
- **Choose What Kind of Examples You Get**: **Lookup examples from** — **All** (the default),
  **Anime**, **Live action** or **YouTube**. Anything other than All filters the results to that
  kind of source, so you can line the lookup up with the sort of material you're actually studying.
  A reading-focused session and an anime session want different examples of the same word.

- **One Site For Now**: Nadeshiko is the only destination in this build. Choosing between different
  lookup *sites* is planned; the address lives in one place so it's a small change when it lands.

## Setup Instructions
1. Extract the zip file.
2. Run `Surasura.exe`.

## UPDATE INSTRUCTIONS
> **On v2.0 or later?** This is a one-click in-app update. You'll see **⬆ Update available** at the
> bottom-left of the dashboard — click **Update now** and Surasura reopens on v2.1.2. Your known
> words, ignore/blacklist/graduated lists, content, results, settings and library order are never
> touched.

If you're on **v1.9 or earlier**, or you'd rather update by hand:

- Download and unzip the new release.
- Move your **User Files** (Ignore list, freq lists, blacklist, GraduatedList) into `User Files/<language>/`.
- Move your **content** into `data/<language>/`.

**Note:** Back up your User Files and data before updating manually.
