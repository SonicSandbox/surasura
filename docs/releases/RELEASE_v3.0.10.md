## What's better

- **The journey names shows and episodes.** Its sidebar, *Target met* and Zen's header say *Hoshikuzu Station · Ep 1*,
  not `Hoshikuzu Station S01E01.ja.srt` (the file name is still in the hover).
- **A word's *Score* is now its *Worth*,** and the reference-list badge (*G-1*) is gone.
- **順 Junban leads with its question:** *Put your new cards in the order you'll meet them?* sits above its options.
- **Send report** says so when your included lists name your library's titles.
- ***Use every core*** is offered on setup's load screen, once it finds a big library (64 files or more), and is saved
  the moment you flip it.
- **hato: *Next in line…*** A video waiting behind another hato search says so, then *Looking for subtitles…*.
- **The AI voice (beta)** keeps its time per sentence in a hover instead of in sight.

## Updating

**On 3.0.0 to 3.0.9?** Surasura offers 3.0.10 by itself: press **Update now**. It updates in place; your library,
words and settings stay as they are.

**Piyo's helper running from 3.0.7 or earlier** still holds this one update: the update window asks you to **Stop it**
once. From 3.0.8 on, it steps aside by itself.

## Coming from 2.5

1. Run **`Surasura-3.0.10-setup.exe`** (no admin needed). Your **known words, word lists and settings** come with you;
   your 2.5 folder is left exactly as it was.
2. Add your shows' and books' folders in setup — a 2.x tier keeps its place (NOW → Current, 6+ Months → Goal).
3. Your first Generate re-reads your library once. After that, the usual short ones.

Stop using 2.5 once 3.0 runs (the two could write the same files). Everything new in 3.0:

<details>
<summary><b>Known limits</b></summary>

- **Desktop Anki must be open** for cards to be made and ordered. Chinese cards need Anki Miner 3.12 or later.
- On a **very large library** (20,000+ files), the list can hitch for a moment as a new episode comes in.
- Windows only. The installer isn't signed: Windows may say *Windows protected your PC* → **More info** →
  **Run anyway**.

</details>
