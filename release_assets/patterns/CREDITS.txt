Counts of how Japanese words are used (particles, forms, word pairs, frames), computed by Surasura
(builder version 7) from:

  * RealPersonaChat — Yamashita et al. (2023), nu-dialogue, CC BY-SA 4.0.
    https://github.com/nu-dialogue/real-persona-chat
  * Aozora Bunko — public-domain works, via globis-university/aozorabunko-clean (CC BY 4.0).
    https://www.aozora.gr.jp/  https://huggingface.co/datasets/globis-university/aozorabunko-clean
  * Japanese Wikipedia — Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0 (dump of 2026-09-20).
    https://ja.wikipedia.org/
  * Leipzig Corpora Collection — Japanese newscrawl 2015, 2016, 2018 and 2019, © Universität Leipzig /
    Sächsische Akademie der Wissenschaften / InfAI, CC BY 4.0. Counted into word statistics only.
    https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/  — D. Goldhahn, T. Eckart & U. Quasthoff: Building Large
    Monolingual Dictionaries at the Leipzig Corpora Collection: From 100 to 200 Languages. LREC 2012.

Changes: the texts were split into words (fugashi + UniDic-lite) and only counts, short word
pairings and spellings of words seen at least 20 times are kept; no sentences are included.
